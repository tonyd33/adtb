## pricing

- Maker plan is free: 1 user, single currency, search, meta-parts, BOM import, label printing, barcode scanning, custom fields, KiCad integration. [V 2026-10-26] https://partsbox.com/pricing.html
- Paid plans billed annually: Essentials EUR 49/month (2 users), Production EUR 99/month (3 users), Control EUR 239/month (5 users, lot control, serials), Compliance EUR 499/month (8 users, audit trail, 21 CFR Part 11); extra users EUR 12/month. [V 2026-10-26] https://partsbox.com/pricing.html
- No on-premise or offline option is listed; hosted web service only. [V 2026-10-26] https://partsbox.com/pricing.html
- Review aggregators list older USD tiers (Lab/Workshop $59/month, Production $109, Company $259, Medical/Pharma $549). [S 2026-10-26] https://www.capterra.com/p/169152/PartsBox/pricing/
