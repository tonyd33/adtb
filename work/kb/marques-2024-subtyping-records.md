# Marques, Florido, Vasconcelos, Algebraic subtyping for extensible records (2024)

## Records

- Extends Simple-sub (Parreaux, ICFP 2020, an easier reformulation of MLsub inference) with row variables for extensible records, keeping decidable inference; ongoing work, soundness and completeness proofs not done. MLsub records are not extensible. [V 2026-10-25] arXiv 2407.06747v1 abstract, sec. 1
