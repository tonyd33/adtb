# EU e-invoicing mandates

kind: format
home: https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-digital-age-vida_en

## timeline

- Belgium: structured B2B e-invoices over Peppol (BIS Billing 3.0, UBL) required for all VAT-registered businesses from 1 January 2026, no size exemption. [S 2026-10-26] https://originstamp.com/en/blog/reader/eu-e-invoicing-mandates-timeline-2026-2030
- Germany: all B2B businesses must receive e-invoices since 1 January 2025; issuing required from January 2027 above EUR 800k revenue, everyone from January 2028. [S 2026-10-26] https://originstamp.com/en/blog/reader/eu-e-invoicing-mandates-timeline-2026-2030
- France: reception for all from September 2026; issuance phased by size, small and micro businesses by September 2027 or 2028 (sources disagree). [S 2026-10-26] https://www.facturwise.com/en/blog/e-invoicing-deadlines-eu-country-timeline
