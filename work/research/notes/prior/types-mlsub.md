---
title: Dolan and Mycroft, MLsub (POPL 2017)
---
HM extended with subtyping: types form a distributive lattice, joins only in output positions and meets only in input positions (polar types), inference by biunification with principal types, and decidable subsumption for checking an inferred type against a signature. Records get width and depth subtyping; the join of two record types keeps their common fields. Simple-sub (Parreaux, ICFP 2020) reformulates the algorithm. https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/bitstream/handle/1810/261583/Dolan_and_Mycrof-2017-POPL-AM.pdf
