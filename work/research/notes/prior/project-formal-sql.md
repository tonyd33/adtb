---
title: Formal semantics of SQL (HoTTSQL, SQLCoq, Guagliardo and Libkin)
---
HoTTSQL: Coq denotational semantics over K-relations and univalent
types, used to prove optimizer rewrites. SQLCoq (Benzaken and
Contejean): executable Coq semantics of realistic SQL with NULLs,
proven equivalent to a bag algebra, validated against PostgreSQL and
Oracle. Guagliardo and Libkin: paper semantics implemented in Python as
an oracle, matched PostgreSQL and Oracle on 100,000 random queries.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.04822
