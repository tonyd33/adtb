---
title: QUIC streams and HTTP/2 flow control
---
QUIC: independent bidirectional and unidirectional streams, flow
control per stream and per connection, stream count limits, 0-RTT
without replay protection. gRPC backpressure comes from HTTP/2
windows.
https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9000.html
https://grpc.io/docs/guides/flow-control/
