---
title: NRC, Kleisli and CPL
---
Nested relational calculus: comprehensions over sets, bags and lists
treated uniformly as monads, with structural recursion restricted to
well-defined forms. CPL, the Kleisli surface, writes `{x | \x <- R, p}`
with `{}`, `{| |}` and `[ ]` for the three kinds. Buneman, Naqvi, Tannen,
Wong, TCS 149(1), 1995, https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3975(95)00024-Q;
Wong, "Kleisli, a functional query system", JFP 10(1), 2000,
https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1017/S0956796899003585
