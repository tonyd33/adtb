Budiu et al., PVLDB 16(7) 2023. A database is a stream of snapshots indexed by transaction; Q^Δ = D ∘ ↑Q ∘ I. Linear operators are their own increment, joins are bilinear, the chain rule incrementalizes a plan operator by operator. Z-sets carry signed weights; distinct, join, I and D hold O(|DB|) state; MIN needs the full set. Recursion and stratified negation included. https://docs.feldera.com/vldb23.pdf
