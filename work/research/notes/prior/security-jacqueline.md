---
title: Jacqueline (PLDI 2016)
---
Policy-agnostic programming: policies sit on model fields, take the row
and the viewer; faceted execution keeps a secret and a public facet.
The faceted ORM stores each facet as a row tagged by `jid` and `jvars`,
so ordinary selects and joins carry facets.
https://people.seas.harvard.edu/~chong/pubs/pldi16-jacqueline.pdf
