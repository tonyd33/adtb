title: PRISM, PRISM++ and BiDEL schema modification operators
---
Curino et al., VLDB 2008 and PVLDB 4 (2010): SMOs such as `PARTITION
TABLE`, `MERGE TABLE`, `DECOMPOSE`, `JOIN`, with inverses or a flag that
an operator is not information preserving; PRISM++ rewrites legacy
queries and updates and evolves integrity constraints. Herrmann et al.,
InVerDa (SIGMOD 2017): BiDEL `SPLIT`/`MERGE` as bidirectional operators,
every schema version served as views over shared tables, readable and
writable. PRISM details from search snippets only.
