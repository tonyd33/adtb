title: Graefe, Modern B-Tree Techniques
---
Survey (FnT Databases 3(4), 2011). Normalized keys compared by memcmp,
null bit per column, terminated strings. Prefix truncation from fence
keys, suffix truncation of separators, poor man's normalized keys,
ghost records, B-link trees as two-step splits. Merged indexes
interleave several indexes in one B-tree, with domain tags and the
index identifier inside the normalized key. Härder's combined image
puts per-table counters and RID lists under one key.
