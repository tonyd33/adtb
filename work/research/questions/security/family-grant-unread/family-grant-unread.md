  refuse-when-callables:

The ruling security/family-signature/unanimous compares the signatures of live bundles. A live `web-1.0` may have a header only, from a failed open, or callable rows without the text they came from.

## security/family-grant-unread/refuse-when-callables

A header alone declares nothing. A bundle with callables and no signature may declare the query, and covering some live bundles silently and not others is what the ruling forbids. Presenting it at an open or retiring it releases the refusal.

## security/family-grant-unread/skip

The grant always goes through and may silently miss a live bundle.
