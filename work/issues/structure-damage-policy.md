## 2026-11-09, step 4b landed

Storage no longer marks at detection in session. The checkpoint's fold meeting a damaged list page and a read meeting a damaged LSN map page record the damage and refuse what met it; the record maps through the table at the next call end, commit or open (`holders_meeting` answers `DamageList` and `LsnMap` before the walk). At the open, list and map pages are marked through `map_open_fault`. `Pager::quarantine` takes `&Ordered` and the mapper is its only production caller; a hold is reached only through `hold_quarantine(&Opening)`; the mapper reads `Pager::phase()`, its one phase source, and `Engine::of` ends the open before background maintenance starts. Ruled: an unreadable list page met in session marks nothing, holding the database only when the open meets it (storage.md §6; policy and search tables follow). Dropping the in-session hold let the checkpointer recycle the log of records that could not fold, so the checkpoint's redo LSN is now `min(dirty rec_lsn, Evidence::redo_bound())`: the first record since the last logged fold, kept through the fold until its change of the list is logged (`folding`, `fold_logged`; a concurrent checkpoint waits on the list latch meanwhile); recovery replays damage, free and image records from the redo LSN, and an open that finds no list page keeps the bound for every replayed record (`list_unread`). storage.md §6, §7 and §8 (ordering covers every mark) amended. `mark_setters` damage_log.rs 3 to 1, lsnmap.rs 1 to 0. Tickets filed: unfoldable-list-pins-log, map-holder-walks-whole-map. `KNOWN` 81. Next: step 5.
