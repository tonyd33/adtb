## 2026-11-08, from the version-owner design

The scoped snapshot: a registered reader's snapshot holds back only its plan's heaps, and nothing checks that it reads only those (`Scope::Heaps`); a wrong scope lets vacuum free a version the reader then walks. Lead ruling (structure-version-owner Q7): the `Reader` carries the `Scope` it was taken with and `Store` checks each heap against it, in this program, after structure-version-owner step 2 adds the `Reader`.

## 2026-11-10, design and rulings

Design report: work/briefs/design-transactions-r1-report.md (local). Program of 13 steps; order 1 and 2 first, 3 after 1, 4 after 3, 5 after 1 and 3, 6 after 5, 7 independent, 8 then 10, 9 and 11 after 10, 12 and 13 independent. Steps 4, 8, 9, 11 and 13 change client-visible behaviour; the rest do not.

Lead rulings:

- Q1, the transaction guard: an owned guard. The transaction resources (`txns`, `ser`, `readers`, `waits`, the dead counts) move into a `write::Session` behind one `Arc` that the `Txn` holds, so a server `Call` can keep its `Txn` across worker steps. `abort` splits into an infallible in-memory release (hold, sid, readers, waits, dead counts, the xid out of `running`) and the fallible commit-log record. `commit(self)` and `abort(self)` consume and mark the guard ended first; drop releases and records the abort best effort, counting a failure. An `Err` in `commit` aborts and returns the commit's error. A failed abort record leaves the commit log saying in progress, which readers treat as not committed and recovery settles as aborted; mvcc.md §4.1 and §9 state it in step 3. Reason: a borrowing guard cannot live in a resumable call, a whole-engine `Arc` hides what the abort depends on, and an orphan list leaves the watermark pinned until a later call.
- Q2, panics caught as findings: reindex catches only the failures its job returns as `Err`; a panic ends the process, as fatal-split rules, after step 3. Scrub keeps catching, every resource inside the catch a guard, until typed shape faults cover what it reads; ticketed separately. Reason: a reindex panic is an engine invariant and the old index stays visible; scrub's purpose is to read arbitrarily bad pages.
- Q3, lock preconditions: positive preconditions become witness parameters (`admit_commit(&Ordered)`, `rebuild(&VacuumHold)`, a `BundleWriter` guard replacing `bundle_writer: Lock<()>`); negative ones are entry assertions under `latch-checks` from the pager's per-thread latch count and a ser-holder id; `write::Database`'s four locks get ranks checked as `heap::Stage` checks latches. Positive first.
- Q4, the error spine: a `Class` on `write::Error` decided by variant (operating, damage, refused by a mark, checker guarantee, engine invariant); `ends_transaction`, `fail`'s mapping and `of_mapped` read it. `write::Error::Exec(exec::Error)` replaces the copied arms; `Transactions(mvcc::Error)` typed; one `From<catalog::Error>` lifts damage to `Page`; engine-state `Broken` variants panic naming their invariant. Not one shared enum: it would couple the executor to write-path variants.
- Q5, executor inputs: one private-field constructor per kind of read (serializable, safe, past, migrating), each building its heaps from `program.reads` and failing with `Internal` naming a missing binding instead of panicking.
- Q6, the plan validator: always on, at the end of `plan::lower`, refusing with `plan::Broken`, for P1, P3, P5, P6, P8, P9 and the `Fix` and `Top` shapes. Step 7 measures its cost against planning; past 5% of planning time on the planner tests it reports before landing. Reason: one linear walk, and it replaces release-build panics on a planner bug.
- Q7, scope: `Transactions::take(scope)` returns a `Hold` guard; the `Reader` carries its scope and keeps its hold alive; `Store` refuses a heap outside the scope with `Internal`. Scoped publishing for registered calls (mvcc.md §9) is a later feature.

## 2026-11-10, step 1 landed

Landed ca206853, full check exit 0 (3617 tests). `Transactions::take(scope)` returns an owned `Hold` guard with its snapshot; the guard reaches the transactions state through a `Weak<dyn Release>`, so it does not keep the engine alive and needs no generic parameter, and drop releases the hold once on every path. `Transactions::release` is gone, `Holds::release` is private to mvcc and `SnapshotId` is not exported: releases by id outside mvcc 16 to 0. The snapshot's commit position moved into the guard as `Taken`, so a read-only `Txn` dropped without abort still logs its safe marker. Commit releases its hold just after the ser lock, before `wait_durable` (holding longer is conservative); abort under it; mvcc.md §9 states both and that a read-write transaction's hold is released only after `log_safe` runs for it. No `?` between `take` and release was reachable; the enforcer is `mvcc::clog_tests::a_dropped_hold_releases_its_heaps`, failing on main. Protection narrowed: a `write::Txn` dropped without ending now releases its hold, while its xid, sid, readers entry and waits leak until step 3.

## 2026-11-10, step 2 landed

Landed 6101a53c, full check exit 0 (3624 tests). `Transactions::begin` returns a `Begun` guard (through `Weak<dyn Release>`, as `Hold`); `commit` and `abort` take it by value and mark it ended first; a drop aborts best effort. Abort is split: the id always leaves `running`, and the commit-log record is attempted, a failure counted (`unrecorded_aborts`, testing only) and not returned; a thread that is panicking records nothing. A failed commit returns its own error. Ids that left `running` without a record stay in `State::unresolved`, which bounds each reservation's `resolved` and vacuum's `clean_below` (`Transactions::resolved_below`); mvcc.md §4.1, §4.3, §9 and §10 and the `Snapshot` invariant say resolved or unresolved, an unresolved id treated as not committed. The pager has a closed state set first in `Pager::close`; `Transactions::admit` refuses after it (`mvcc::Error::Closed`), so a guard dropped after close releases in memory and writes nothing (embedding tests at server/embedding.rs reach that path).

Reproduced and fixed (class-not-atomic): a migration or an index release failing on an unreadable catalog root, which marks nothing, left its id running and the watermark pinned until restart (write_states_tests, both failing on main). `txns.begin(` returning a bare `Xid` outside mvcc 6 to 0; `?` windows between begin and end 4 to 0. A dropped `write::Txn` now aborts its xid; its sid, readers entry and waits still leak until step 3.

## 2026-11-10, step 3 landed

Landed 4248d1a5, full check exit 0 (3636 tests). The transaction resources (pager handle, transactions, serializer, readers, waits, dead counts) live in a `write::Session` behind one `Arc` that `write::Database` and every `Txn` hold, the `Txn` through `Arc<dyn Abandon>` so it stays non-generic and `Send`. `commit` and `abort` call `Txn::end` first; an unended `Txn` dropped aborts and releases its `Begun`, serializer entry, readers entry, hold, waits and dead counts, logging safe snapshot markers unless the thread is panicking or the pager is closed. A panic inside commit or abort after `end` reaches `Ended`'s drop, which releases what the end had not; the serializer entry has its own flag, cleared as soon as `ser.committed` or `ser.aborted` returns, so it is never ended twice. `Serializer::aborted` takes the ended id out of `found_safe`, so a reader dropped without logging leaves no stale sid. mvcc.md §4.5 states the `Txn` contract and its lock order; §9 restated for no-log drops.

Reproduced and fixed (class-not-atomic): a panic in reindex's scan under its snapshot left the read `Txn`'s serializer and readers entries; a server `Call` dropped holding its read left both; a planted insert failing after begin left its serializer entry and waits. Each test failed on main. Production `Txn` with no `Drop` 1 to 0. A `testing` counter of ends by drop is asserted zero at each engine close (before `open` is cleared) and each `test_world::WritePath` drop, skipped on early-return closes, panicking drops, `into_db`, crash rounds and drops during scheduler shutdown and `stop_reindexing`; the deliberate drops each assert their count. `VerifySnapshot` keeps its own drop (as a newtype every verify would count as a drop). Accepted: a commit panicking after its record but before `ser.committed` counts its inserts as dead (vacuum scheduling only); a live `Txn` keeps the pager and its buffer pool alive past close. Rulings for step 4: a drop after the `Txn`'s own operation returned an error is intended and not counted; commit's failure returns the commit's error.

## 2026-11-10, Q1 amended for step 4

A failed commit, or any operation that fails and then aborts, returns its own error unless the abort's commit-log record fails; then the call's reply is the record's error as `fail` maps it. Every way the record can fail (damage the call found, an I/O failure, the database already refused, the engine closed) is the fact the spec makes that call's reply: the call meeting damage fails with it, calls after an unreadable status page fail as `Storage`, a closed engine is `Rejected Closed`. One write-path function takes the cause and returns the reply, so no site picks between the two errors. Reason: step 4 found that returning the cause there would hide damage the call found, and that an unreadable status page is `Storage`, not a quarantine, so no rule narrower than "the record's failure wins" keeps main's replies.

## 2026-11-10, step 4 landed

Landed 9dd6f105, full check exit 0 (3643 tests). Every site that ends a transaction after a failure goes through one gate, `write::Database::abandon(tx, cause, reply) -> Result<E, E>`: it releases what the transaction holds, records the abort best effort, forms the cause only once the record is written and returns it, else counts the failed record (`Transactions::count_unrecorded`) and returns the record's failure mapped by `reply`. `Engine::abandon` maps through `write_error`; a failed commit returns its own error unless its abort record fails. After-failure aborts in api and write 25 to 0; a new structure-check row `api_aborts` counts the remaining success-path `.abort(` calls in api (mod.rs 1, server/call.rs 1, txn.rs 2), counts only falling. `Txn` carries a failure mark set by `Txn::noted` where its operations return `Err` (not on `Error::Blocked`); a drop of a marked `Txn` is not counted as an end by drop. `AdHocTransaction` stores the cause for later calls and gives the failing call the record's reply. mvcc.md §4.5 states the gate, the counted drops and where the count is asserted.

Client-visible, by the amended Q1: a server call whose failed abort record meets a pending stop request now settles the record's failure (`Fatal Storage` or `Quarantined`) where main settled the request's reply (`Rejected Cancelled` and the like); its test fails on main. Every other reply is as on main; the failed records are now counted. Left: the call's commit arm and a role call's audit row can still replace a failed record's reply, filed as call-commit-record-replaced-by-stop.

## 2026-11-10, Q2 landed

## 2026-11-10, step 5 landed

Landed 2f7bd77a, full check exit 0 (3647 tests). A `Reader` is built only by `Transactions::reader(pager, &Hold, me)` or `reader_at(pager, &Pinned)` and keeps a share of the hold or pin, which is released when its last holder drops; `Transactions::take(scope)` returns only the `Hold`, and `pin`/`pin_time` return a `Pinned` guard (the public `unpin` is gone). `Txn.snapshot` is gone; write-path readers come from `tx.hold()`. The store refuses a heap its reader does not cover with `Internal(Broken::OutsideScope(heap))`: `Store::heap`, `past_heap`, `fetch` (every heap of the relation's sum), and every index read through `Store::entries`, whose `probe` and `range` now take the route heaps (`exec::Routes`); `fetch_past` checks its route and history heaps before probing. A reader at a pinned marker covers every heap. Readers built from a bare `Snapshot` outside mvcc 22 to 0 by type. No read outside a scope or after release was reachable on main. mvcc.md §9 states that a reader keeps its hold, the drop precondition (no page latch, not the transactions' state lock), and that a reader outliving its `Txn` (the server call's error arm, queued morsel tasks) holds the watermark back; nothing by type stops a non-recording reader reading after its end. Left: readers scanning heaps outside any store check no scope, filed as unscoped-heap-scans.

## 2026-11-10, step 6 landed

Landed cb0c2a8e, full check exit 0 (3649 tests). A store is built only through exec's constructors, one per kind of read: `Store::serializable` (the `Txn`'s reader, serializer and sid), `safe` (a verify snapshot), `migrating` (catalog validation, records nothing) and `past` (a pinned marker, built from the `at` block's reads). They take mvcc parts, not write types, so exec does not depend on write; the write side ties each kind to its argument (`Database::store`, `VerifySnapshot::store`, `Pasts::store`, validation). `exec::start` runs each program over `Store::narrowed(program.reads())`, history included, so every execution reads only what its plan lists; a heap or index outside the listing is refused with `Internal` (`UnlistedHeap`, `UnlistedIndex`), a listed read with no binding with `UnboundHeap`/`UnboundIndex`; the write path's own direct reads use the full store. The database's heap and index maps are `Arc`s in one `exec::Bindings`, so no statement clones them. Struct literals of `Store`/`AtSnapshot`/`exec::Call` outside exec 12 to 0; their `pub` fields 15 to 0; `panic!("the store holds` 2 to 0; `plan::AtRead`/`exec::At` carry their indexes. No statement read anything its plan omits. Ruled for step 7: a fetch reads the heaps of every constructor of the route's relation, which `Plan.reads` does not list today; P8 widens to list them and execution.md drops the exception documented now. Kind safety (a safe or migrating store over a recording reader) rests on the write-side callers, not the type.
