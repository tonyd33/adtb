## 2026-11-08, design and rulings

Design: work/briefs/design-version-owner-r1-report.md (local), eleven steps; this program is steps 1 to 10. Steps 1 to 6 change no behaviour: (1) `mvcc::Writers` replaces the header's raw `xmin`/`xmax`; (2) a `Reader` returning `Seen { visible, before }` held by `AtSnapshot`, `AtMarker` and the raw `sees` callers; (3) `Horizon`, `Fate` and one `fate` in mvcc, called by vacuum and history; (4) one `chain::roots`, `Writers::precedes`, a fallible `walk` that returns the version; (5) one `HeapWrite` that takes latches in rank order and owns the all-visible flag, VM bit, directory category, large-value retain and release, and settle; (6) `HeapPage`/`Listed` views over the guard's owner, `From<Tid>` for `WriteTarget` test-only. Steps 7 to 10 change behaviour, each with its pinning case: (7) `raw` answers `Changed` instead of asserting the owner; (8) an unreserved xid is refused, not a panic; (9) `version::link` requires `precedes`; (10) `retag` and catalog inserts prune on access.

Lead rulings: (Q1) heap imports mvcc's value types `Writers` and `Fate`, never `Transactions`; mvcc imports nothing of heap. (Q2) every heap insert path prunes on access (step 10); mvcc.md §10.2 is not narrowed. (Q3) an unreserved xid in a header is damage of the page holding it, refused through the damage the read path already reports for a structural fault; a typed shape-fault damage variant is a separate storage ticket, not this program. (Q4) a version moved to history is identified by its key and its writers, stated in mvcc.md §10.3; `Writers: Eq` implements it. (Q5) `version::link` takes the writers check (step 9). (Q6) the roots heap's hand-numbered constructors and `naming_sequences` leave this program for structure-catalog-roots. (Q7) the scoped-snapshot check belongs to structure-transactions; the `Reader` leaves room for its `Scope`.

## 2026-11-08, step 1 landed

A version's writers are `mvcc::Writers { inserter, deleter: Option<Xid> }` with private fields (mvcc/writers.rs); `heap::Header` is `{ writers, next }`, the large-value chunk header's writer is an `Xid`, and heap write calls take `Xid`. `Snapshot::sees`, `Transactions::sees`, `probe_unique`, `check_write` and `read_before` take `&Writers`. The successor rule is `Writers::precedes` (`follows`, `link_faults`, verify), the only deleter clear is `Writers::cleared` (`restore`, prune), `supersede` takes a function over `Writers`, and `mark_kept`, `history::held` and exec's `Passed` compare by `Writers`. The 16-byte layout is unchanged. Counts: `.xmin`/`.xmax` outside mvcc 74 prod and 60 test lines to 0, and structure-check's `xmin_xmax` row is deleted; `Xid(` outside mvcc 19 to 4; heap `Header {` literals outside version.rs 5 to 0 (a grep count; the fields stay `pub`). Behaviour differs only on damage: a predecessor with no deleter linking to a successor of inserter 0 now gets the `link_faults` fault "hot-updated but names no heap-only successor" (pinned by a unit test) and `follows` ends the walk there. Getters that still decide rules, each removed later: `vacuum::judge`, `history::movable`, `judge_past`, `catalog::latest` (step 3); `exec::store::writer_of` and `scan_past` (step 2); verify's chain gate and `deleter >= reserved` (step 8). mvcc.md §3 states that the successor rule, the deleter clear and a version's identity are operations of `Writers`; §10.3 states Q4's identity. Next: step 2.

## 2026-11-08, step 2 landed

`mvcc::Reader` (mvcc/reader.rs) is the one visibility operation: `sees(&Writers)` returns `Seen`, the verdict and the writer it turned on, testing the snapshot's in-progress set before any commit-log read; `Reader::before(seen)` gives the writer a serializable read depends on, reading a status only for a writer the snapshot excludes and only to drop an aborted one, and only `AtSnapshot::saw` with a serial calls it, so readers with no serial (past reads, verify, check_rows, reindex) read the commit log exactly where they did before. `probe_unique(me, ..)` and `check_write(me, ..)` take the writer's id. The reader is generic over a `Statuses` source, so the table-driven model tests drive the production code; `visibility_matches_a_model_of_random_histories` and the commit-log random-history test check `(visible, before)` against their models, floored on both branches. Deleted: `Snapshot::sees`, `Transactions::sees`, the free `probe_unique`, `check_write` and `read_before`, `writer_of`, `depend_all`, `AtSnapshot::depends`; exec's `Reads` has one `saw`. Counts: raw `.sees(` with operands outside mvcc 8 prod (16 with tests) to 0; status closures outside mvcc 10 to 6 (vacuum 3, history 2, verify 1); `fn writer_of` 0. One behaviour change, cleared by review against mvcc.md §8.2: SIREAD reads are recorded per version as read, earlier than the per-batch set before, so no rw-dependency is missed or late. Getters still deciding rules: vacuum, history and `catalog::latest` (step 3), verify's chain gate (step 8), `copy_version`'s inserter (step 5). Next: step 3.

## 2026-11-08, step 3 landed

`Transactions::fate(pager, &Writers, &Horizon)` (mvcc/fate.rs) is the one statement of a version's fate: `Remove`, `AllVisible`, `Keep` or `Clear`, under a `Horizon` whose rule is `Current`, `Moving` or `Past`. `Horizon` has private fields and is built only by `horizon_now(heap)` and `horizon_fixed(heap, cutoff, rule)` (F5: at most the oldest running transaction). `kept_by_window` and `fate` share one window rule; `resolved` replaces the sweep's closure; `latest_live` replaces `catalog::latest`'s predicate (now `latest_rows`, which only collects); `Transactions::judge` is the only producer of heap's prune closure. vacuum's private `Mode` is the only source of a `Watermarks` rule, so `Moving` without history heaps cannot be built. Deleted: `vacuum::insert`, `vacuum::update`, `vacuum::Horizon`, `judge`, `judge_within`, `history::movable`, `history::judge_past`, `heap::Fate`; history.rs no longer imports vacuum, so the import cycle is gone. Counts: the step 3 grep 6 to 0; `Status::` outside mvcc 10 to 1 (verify, step 8); status closures outside mvcc 6 to 1. `fate_matches_a_model_of_random_histories` runs a db with history under sim time and draws all three rules: under `Current`, `Remove` implies the model's window does not keep the version; `Current` and `Moving` differ exactly on window-kept versions; under `Past`, `Keep` iff the deleter committed at or after the cutoff; floored on every (rule, fate) pair by distinct versions. mvcc.md §9 states that a snapshot never outlives its reader's transaction. Getters still deciding rules: verify's chain gate (step 8), `copy_version`'s inserter (step 5). Next: step 4.

## 2026-11-08, step 4 landed

heap/chain.rs owns the chain rules: `start`, `from` and `roots` state C3 once (used by `prune::plan`, `scan_rooted`, `chain_rows` and a new `Heap::hot_run`, which replaces write/apply.rs's); `link`, `chain`, `link_faults` and `follows` moved there; verify calls `successor(pager, sum, &Link)`, which applies C2 and refuses a successor reached through a redirect, and `header_at` is deleted. `walk` takes a fallible predicate over each link and its decoded row, decodes each one-page stretch (row and out-of-line values) under that page's shared latch, runs the predicate after the latch drops, remembers visited versions across pages, and returns the accepted version. Fix, class-not-atomic: a read of the past walked, dropped the latch and read the row again with `.expect("a walked version is there")`, so `vacuum_keeping` moving the version between the two panicked (and a pruned and reused slot could return another version's row). Reproduced on main by `history_tests::a_fetch_of_the_past_returns_the_version_it_walked_to_when_vacuum_moves_it` and its out-of-line variant (a 9000-byte value, asserted out of line), both panicking at exec/store.rs:628 on main; the type now returns the row, so no caller reads it twice. Counts: `failed = Some` 2 to 0; the walked-version expect 1 to 0; `fn hot_run` in apply.rs 0; chain-start rules 4 to 1; heap/handle `disallowed_methods` expects 9 to 8. Damage-only differences, all stricter: a redirect to a redirect or a self-redirect ends the walk; a visited version ends it. Accepted: `link_faults` does not use `roots`, since it checks hot slots no root reaches. The same read-again shape at write/apply.rs:132 and 436 is safe by the writer's watermark and belongs to W9 (structure-transactions). Next: step 5.
